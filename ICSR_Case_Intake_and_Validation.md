Seriousness is dependent on clinical seriousness criteria.
Expectedness is listed in the safety reference.
Neither indicate causality.
Seriousness does not necessarily mean severity.
Unexpected doesn’t necessarily mean caused by suspected product.

9. Conclusion
This task shows how adverse event data is collected, structured and validated to create Individual Case Safety Reports (ICSRs).
4 cases were analysed. 2 cases (PV-102 and PV-104) were serious. 2 cases (PV-101 and PV-103) were not serious.
The analysis identified missing patient, reporter, medication and clinical information. Questions were prepared to help with completeness.
This task highlights the importance of accurate documentation of cases, determining seriousness, expectedness and validating data.
